This work examines emerging technologies and the digital economy, including their vulnerabilities, the economic value of open government data, firms' dynamic capabilities during crises, and competition in digital markets.

**Funding:** Department of Defense MINDS program, Co-applicant, 2019–2021, *Security-Policy Nexus of Emerging Technologies* ($750,000).
