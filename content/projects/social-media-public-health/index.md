This programme of work uses statistical models and text analytics on online data to study public health communication and public response, including studies of Zika, COVID-19 and vaccine hesitancy.

**Chair:** Concordia Research Chair in Business Analytics for Public Health and Policy, 2023 (emerging scholar, $100,000).
